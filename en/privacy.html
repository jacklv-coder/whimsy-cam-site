Your creations stay on your phone. Here’s how Whimsy Cam uses device permissions and handles your data.

01No personal data collection

Whimsy Cam does not send any personal data to the developer. The app has no account registration, ads, statistics or analytics SDKs, tracking, or social features. It does not access your location. The optional in-app purchase (Whimsy Cam Pro) is paid through Apple; the developer never receives your payment details, and whether you've bought it is checked with Apple on your phone.

02Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only to take photos and record video, including sound when recording. Images are processed locally on your phone and are not uploaded.

03Photos, videos and library access

Your photos, videos and stickers are kept in the app’s own local storage. When you save to Photos, the app requests add-only access: it can add your creations but cannot read your photo library.

Importing uses the system photo picker. The app can access only the photos or videos you choose yourself.

04Sticker cutouts

Sticker cutouts use the on-device recognition built into iOS. Your images do not leave your phone.

05Font downloads

When you add text to a sticker, iOS may download some fonts from Apple’s servers as needed. This is a system feature. The app requests only the font name; it does not send your text or photos.

06Sharing and copying

Your creations are passed to an app you choose only when you tap Share or Copy.

07Deleting data and backups

You can delete individual creations in the app, or delete the app to clear all its local data. There is no server-side account to delete. Depending on your device settings, iCloud or computer backups may include app data.

08Children

The app does not collect information from any age group.

09This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access logs, such as IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. The developer has not added any analytics or advertising scripts to this website.

10Contact

Please contact us through GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

11Policy changes

When this policy is updated, the effective date on this page will be updated too.
